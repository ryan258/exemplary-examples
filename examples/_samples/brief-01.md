SYNTHETIC SAMPLE: Hollis Street Bakery is a fictional business used only to test the tool.

Hollis Street Bakery is a small neighborhood bakery. It sells a sourdough loaf for $7. It is launching a weekly bread subscription: four loaves a month for $24, picked up on Saturdays.

Who buys: people in the neighborhood who like good bread but buy it on impulse, a loaf here and there.

The hesitation we need the example to answer: "I'd never use it enough to be worth committing to."
