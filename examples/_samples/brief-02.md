SYNTHETIC SAMPLE: Pinecrest Bookkeeping is a fictional business used only to test the tool.

Pinecrest Bookkeeping is a one-person bookkeeping service for small trades businesses (plumbers, electricians, landscapers). It charges $250 a month for monthly bookkeeping: categorizing transactions, reconciling the accounts, and sending a one-page summary by the 10th of each month.

Who buys: owner-operators who currently do their own books on Sunday nights and fall behind in busy season.

The hesitation we need the example to answer: "I can do it myself for free, so why pay $250 a month?"

Facts supplied by the business: the owner-operators we serve typically bill $90 an hour on jobs. Doing the books yourself usually takes about 5 hours a month.
